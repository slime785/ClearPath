Trusted sites for common custody questions

Quick answers and official websites for common custody questions in California. Links open in a new tab.

How do I start a custody or visitation case?

The court's self-help center explains the steps and has the forms you need.

  • California Courts Self-Help Guide
  • Kern County Superior Court: Families and Children
Where do I find court forms?

California court forms are free to download. Kern County also has form packets.

  • California Courts: Forms
  • Kern County Superior Court: Self-Help
Can I get free or low-cost legal help?

Legal aid groups and the court's self-help center can help if you can't afford a lawyer.

  • LawHelpCA
  • Kern County Self-Help Center
How does child support work?

The California Department of Child Support Services has information on opening a case and getting payments.

  • California Child Support Services
Where can I learn about the family court in Kern County?

The Kern County Superior Court has pages on its family law division and what the court can and can't do.

  • Kern County Superior Court: Family Law
  • Kern County Superior Court: Legal Resources
